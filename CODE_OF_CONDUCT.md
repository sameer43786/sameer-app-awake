# Code of Conduct

By: Sameer Ali | Contact: sameer43786@gmail.com

This repository welcomes professional, respectful technical discussion.

## Expected conduct

- Focus on reproducible technical facts and constructive feedback.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Do not post personal, financial, authentication, signing, or other sensitive information.
- Report security issues privately as described in `SECURITY.md`.
- Keep issues and discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, impersonation, doxxing, spam, credential sharing, and publication of another person's confidential information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its participants. Contact sameer43786@gmail.com for conduct concerns that should not be handled publicly.
